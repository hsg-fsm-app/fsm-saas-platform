Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No, there are no setup fees. You only pay the monthly subscription rate for your chosen plan.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, annual billing is available with a 15% discount. Contact us to set up annual billing for your account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, and wire transfers for Enterprise customers.
